# 01: Smart preset — stress-triggered per-ticket review with honest coverage

**What to build:** A `smart` gate preset that spends judging effort where context stress actually occurred, instead of reviewing every ticket or none. Diagnosis from the SpriteForge runs: on strong/well-quantized models a per-ticket review mostly returns minor-only (pure spend), while the tickets that compact mid-build carry the blockers (06: two compactions, 6 blockers across rounds). The preset uses compaction as one stress signal — NOT as a defect oracle — and is deliberately narrow in v1: only the per-ticket review schedule changes. The interaction smoke and goal/structural cadence are explicitly out of scope for suppression, because their failure classes (wiring gaps, cross-ticket goal drift) are orthogonal to context loss and mid-run checkpoints are where correction is cheapest. Read first: AGENTS.md, docs/adr/0014, 0021, 0022, 0025, 0044, 0050, then the seams per step. Everything green every step (ADR 0006). The pieces, in execution order:
1. PRESET, NOT A MODE. `smart` enters the preset surface (GatePreset in src/config/config.ts, preset tables and args in src/config/args.ts, persistence in src/config/run-policy.ts) and resolves to concrete existing modes — `GateMode` stays `full|medium|light|off` and no gate grows a branch. Baseline resolution is light's mapping (`code: "medium"`, `visual: "light"`, `goal: "light"` corrective, `structural: "light"`); the only new runtime behavior is the per-ticket review trigger below. A human's explicit per-gate overrides still win.
2. STRESS PREDICATE. A pure, named function (src/execute/run.ts or a new src/execute/review-schedule.ts) decides whether a ticket's per-ticket review is due: `compactions > 0` across the ticket's merged build-phase telemetry, OR `attempts > 1`, OR a builder session restart during the ticket, OR a verify reconcile ran. Compaction counts come from the already-existing `analyzePhase()` telemetry seam (the same source the ladder and `state.builder.needs_full_context` use) — no new JSONL parsing path. The predicate returns a reason string, never a bare boolean.
3. REVIEW SCHEDULING. In processTicket's review branch (src/execute/run.ts), under `smart` only: a non-stressed ticket skips the review phase, records the reason on TicketState (optional persisted field, normalizeState-tolerant) and in the console, and MUST NOT set `review_ok` or push a verdict — a skipped review is "not run", never a minor-only pass (ADR 0050). A stressed ticket runs the review exactly as light/medium does today (severity thresholds, budget machine, soft-pass untouched).
4. GUARDRAILS (v1 non-goals). Interaction smoke keeps its current cadence: once per committed group boundary, blocking, no stress gating. Goal and structural checkpoints keep their cadence: never suppressed by `smart`. Stress-based *earlier* checkpoint escalation is out of scope here — it becomes its own ticket only after the false-negative measurement below says the signal is trustworthy.
5. COVERAGE HONESTY. report.md gains a smart coverage section: reviews run vs skipped with reasons ("review 7/17 — 10 skipped: no stress"), plus checkpoint counts, so a green run never implies scrutiny it did not get. `railhead status`/`next` show a skipped review as "not run (smart — no stress)", never blank or green-implied. The report also counts FALSE NEGATIVES: a ticket skipped by `smart` that a later group/run-end gate (goal, structural, interaction smoke) subsequently fails or flags — that counter is what earns the right to widen or keep the mode.
6. DOCS. One new ADR (smart preset: stress-triggered review, coverage honesty, interaction smoke is never stress-gated) and updates to src/config/AGENTS.md and src/execute/AGENTS.md where preset resolution and the review schedule are described.
